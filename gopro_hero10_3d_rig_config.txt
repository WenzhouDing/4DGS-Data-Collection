============================================================
  NOTES
============================================================
- GoPro Labs firmware required on all cameras
- Hero 10: LCD must be ON for QR code scanning to work
- ISO locked at 400 — good for controlled indoor lighting.
  If your scene is too dark/bright, adjust both ISO Min and
  ISO Max to the same new value.
- White Balance 4500K is a general indoor default. Measure
  your actual light color temp and adjust if needed:
    Tungsten:       ~3200K
    Fluorescent:    ~4000K
    Daylight LED:   ~5500K
- 180° shutter = 1/120s at 60fps (standard cinematic motion
  blur). For sharper frames with less blur (better for
  computer vision / feature matching), consider 90° or 45°.
- Flat color profile preserves dynamic range. Apply LUT or
  color grade in post.
- High bit rate maximizes quality at the cost of larger files.
- No genlock on Hero 10 — use audio clap sync + timecode
  alignment for frame-accurate multi-cam sync.
